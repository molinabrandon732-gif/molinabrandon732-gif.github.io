About Brandon

Brandon Molina is a creator from Goonellabah.

Growing up, Brandon was bullied almost every day at Goonellabah Public School. It left him quiet for years, but those hard years built the story he tells today, so nobody listening has to feel alone in theirs.

Brandon Beyond is his own world that includes talks on resilience, self-worth and faith.
